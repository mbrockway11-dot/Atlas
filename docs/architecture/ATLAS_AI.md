# Atlas AI

**Project:** Atlas  
**Architecture Version:** Phase II  
**Document:** Atlas AI Specification  
**Status:** Canonical Service Specification

---

# Purpose

Atlas AI is the orchestration layer of the Atlas Cognitive Stack.

Its responsibility is to coordinate deterministic intelligence services, aggregate their outputs, normalize confidence, propagate warnings, and produce a unified intelligence payload for downstream reasoning.

Atlas AI is not a language model.

Atlas AI does not invent knowledge.

Atlas AI organizes, synthesizes, and prepares evidence generated by Atlas services.

---

# Architectural Position

```text
AtlasProfile
        │
        ▼
Core Intelligence Services
        │
        ▼
Atlas AI
        │
        ▼
Reasoning Engine
```

Atlas AI is the final intelligence aggregation layer before scientific reasoning begins.

---

# Primary Responsibilities

Atlas AI is responsible for:

- orchestrating intelligence services
- normalizing service outputs
- aggregating confidence
- propagating warnings
- propagating errors
- building unified payloads
- exposing deterministic metrics

Atlas AI never generates unsupported conclusions.

---

# Inputs

Atlas AI receives:

- AtlasProfile
- execution plan
- requested services

Atlas AI does not receive raw user queries.

The Query Planner resolves those first.

---

# Core Intelligence Services

Current services include:

```text
Profile Report

Narrative

Evidence

Graph Intelligence

Temporal Intelligence

Population Intelligence

Vedic Behavior
```

Future services simply register with Atlas AI.

Atlas AI should not require architectural changes when new intelligence services are added.

---

# Responsibilities by Stage

## Service Orchestration

Atlas AI determines:

- execution order
- service availability
- dependency validation

Each service executes independently.

---

## Result Aggregation

Each completed service contributes:

- observations
- strengths
- risks
- priorities
- warnings
- errors
- confidence
- metrics

Atlas AI combines these into one payload.

---

## Confidence Aggregation

Atlas AI produces one canonical confidence object.

Confidence considers:

- service confidence
- missing services
- warnings
- execution failures
- validation completeness

Atlas AI does not invent confidence.

It aggregates confidence.

---

## Warning Propagation

Every upstream warning is preserved.

Atlas AI never suppresses warnings.

Example:

```text
Temporal Intelligence

↓

Moon nakshatra unresolved

↓

Atlas AI

↓

Reasoning
```

The warning remains visible.

---

## Error Propagation

Errors terminate only the affected service.

Atlas AI continues executing independent services whenever possible.

Example:

```text
Graph Intelligence

Success

Temporal Intelligence

Failure

↓

Atlas AI

↓

Partial Intelligence Payload
```

Graceful degradation is preferred.

---

# Outputs

Atlas AI produces one deterministic payload.

Example:

```text
Atlas AI Payload

Services

Confidence

Warnings

Errors

Metrics

Summary

Recommendations
```

The payload becomes the input for the Reasoning Engine.

---

# Atlas AI Metrics

Atlas AI exposes standardized metrics.

Examples:

- service count
- successful services
- failed services
- warning count
- error count
- strengths
- risks
- priorities
- overall confidence

Metrics support auditing and reproducibility.

---

# Relationship to Canonical Structure

Atlas AI never modifies:

- graph
- topology
- morphology
- resonance
- canonical metrics

Atlas AI interprets existing information.

It never constructs structure.

---

# Relationship to Validation Domains

Validation domains contribute observations.

Example:

```text
Canonical Structure

↓

Vedic Behavior

↓

Atlas AI
```

Validation domains do not override structural intelligence.

Atlas AI integrates them alongside all other evidence.

---

# Relationship to Reasoning

Atlas AI does not reason.

Atlas AI prepares reasoning.

Reasoning begins only after Atlas AI has completed aggregation.

This separation preserves modularity.

---

# Determinism

Atlas AI is deterministic.

Identical service outputs produce identical Atlas AI payloads.

No randomness is permitted.

---

# Dependencies

Atlas AI depends upon:

- AtlasProfile
- Query Planner execution plan
- registered intelligence services
- Confidence Model
- Warning & Error Model

Atlas AI does not depend upon:

- Reasoning Engine
- Hypothesis Engine
- Falsification Engine
- Experiment Planner
- Discovery Engine

---

# Extension Policy

New intelligence services should register through the service registry.

Atlas AI should discover them through configuration rather than hard-coded logic whenever practical.

This minimizes coupling.

---

# Engineering Guarantees

Atlas AI guarantees:

- deterministic orchestration
- deterministic aggregation
- standardized payloads
- confidence propagation
- warning propagation
- graceful degradation
- explainable execution

Every downstream reasoning service depends upon these guarantees.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Atlas AI is intended to become the unified cognitive coordination layer for the Atlas platform.

As Atlas grows, Atlas AI will orchestrate hundreds of deterministic intelligence services while preserving a single canonical interface for reasoning.

Regardless of how many analytical capabilities Atlas acquires, the Reasoning Engine should always consume one standardized Atlas AI payload.

This separation allows the platform to scale without increasing cognitive complexity for downstream systems.
