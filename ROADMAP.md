# Atlas Development Roadmap

**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Project Phase:** Canonical Architecture Complete

---

# Vision

Atlas is being developed as a deterministic structural intelligence platform capable of compiling, analyzing, interpreting, and comparing human structural profiles at individual and population scales.

The long-term objective is to create a reproducible computational framework for structural cognition, temporal dynamics, topology, resonance, and large-scale pattern discovery.

Development follows an architecture-first philosophy.

Every phase builds upon the canonical compiler.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every milestone must:

- Preserve determinism
- Maintain a single source of truth
- Extend—not duplicate—the canonical compiler
- Improve explainability
- Increase reproducibility
- Reduce architectural complexity

# Long-Term Philosophy

Atlas is not being built as a collection of disconnected tools.

It is being developed as a coherent structural intelligence platform with a stable architecture, deterministic computation, transparent reasoning, and extensible scientific methodology.

Every completed phase should make Atlas:

- simpler,
- more explainable,
- more reproducible,
- more scalable,
- and easier to extend.

The roadmap is therefore not only a list of features—it is a commitment to preserving architectural integrity as the platform evolves.

> **Build the compiler. Preserve the contract. Expand the intelligence.**
